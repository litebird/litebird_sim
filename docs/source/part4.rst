Systematic effects
==================

This part documents the instrumental systematic effects that can be injected
into the TOD — gain drifts, detector non-linearity and time constant,
half-wave-plate systematics, and pointing disturbances — together with the
spin-based :math:`h`-maps that encode the orientation information of the
scanning strategy.
